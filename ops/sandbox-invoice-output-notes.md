# Invoice output development notes

The printable HTML is a sandbox-only view of a previously issued invoice record. It is not a PDF or structured electronic invoice.

Before production release, verify issuer and customer identities, tax identifiers, service period, invoice numbering, VAT rules, correction documents, archiving, and applicable German structured e-invoice requirements. Do not use placeholder issuer details for real invoices. The compliance approval gate remains in place.

No production deployment or payment execution is part of this change.
